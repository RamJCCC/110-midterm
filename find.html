Where To Play

One-time purchase, $59.99. No subscription, no season pass, no paid DLC. Cross-play and cross-save work between all platforms.

  • PC: Steam, GOG, Microsoft Store and Game Pass
  • PlayStation 4 and 5
  • Xbox One and Series X|S
  • Nintendo Switch and Switch 2
  • Mac
  • iPhone and iPad (new in October 2026, $19.99)

Before You Buy

The first few hours are slow. The Switch version is the weakest and the iOS version has no multiplayer. Buy it where your friends play.
